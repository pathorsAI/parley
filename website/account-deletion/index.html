Account deletion

Deleted means deleted.

This page explains how to permanently delete your Parley account and every recording synced to it. You can do it yourself inside the app, or ask us to do it if you cannot open the app.

What deletion removes: the account itself, and every recording synced to it — audio files, transcripts, summaries, folders, and usage history. Deletion is permanent and irreversible; there is no backup to restore from.

Deleting from the app

  • iOSOpen Parley → Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm with "Delete my account and personal data".
  • AndroidOpen Parley → tap Account (top right) to open the account sheet → Delete account, then confirm with "Delete my account and data".

Both platforms show a confirmation dialog spelling out what is destroyed and require a second, explicit tap. Afterwards the app returns to the sign-in screen, and any recordings still queued for upload on that device are discarded too.

If you own a shared organization

Deletion is refused while your account still owns a shared organization, because removing it would take its other members' shared recordings with it. Transfer the organization to another member, or delete it, from the desktop app first — then delete your account.

Deleting without installing the app

You do not need to install or reinstall Parley. Email contact@pathors.com from the address your account is registered to, with the subject "Parley account deletion" and the account email in the message. We confirm your identity through that address and then delete the account, with exactly the same scope as in-app deletion.
