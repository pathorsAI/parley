雲端 MCP 連接器

把 Parley 連接到 Claude 或 ChatGPT

加一個網址、登入、按下允許，你的 AI 助理就能直接翻你的會議：找出誰承諾了什麼、某個議題在哪幾場被討論過、上次客戶卡在哪裡。不用匯出，也不用一份一份貼逐字稿。

下載 Parley隱私權政策

它做什麼

Parley 雲端在 https://api.parley.tw/mcp 提供一個遠端 MCP 伺服器。把它加進 AI 助理之後，助理可以在你提問時，自己去列出、搜尋並讀取你同步到 Parley 雲端的錄音——包括完整的逐字稿（標上講者與時間），以及 Parley 存下的分析（摘要、重點、待辦）。

這個連接是唯讀的：助理不能修改、刪除或新增任何錄音。它也只讀得到你自己的錄音，和你所屬組織裡分享的錄音。

和桌面版的本機 MCP 不一樣。桌面版內建一個只在你電腦上跑的 MCP 伺服器，適合 Claude Code 這類本機工具（見用 MCP 整理會議筆記）。雲端連接器則不需要開著電腦，在網頁版與手機上的 Claude 也能用，但只看得到已經同步到雲端的錄音。

開始之前

  • 一個 Parley 雲端帳號。就是你在 App 裡登入的那個帳號（電子郵件、Google 或 Apple 登入都可以）。
  • 錄音已經同步到雲端。在 App 裡登入後，錄音會自動同步。只存在裝置上、還沒上傳成功的錄音，助理看不到。
  • 一個支援自訂連接器的 AI 助理。目前是 Claude（網頁版、桌面版或手機版）。團隊或企業方案可能需要由管理員先加入連接器。

在 Claude 加入連接器

  1. 打開 Claude，進入 Settings → Connectors（設定 → 連接器）。
  2. 點 Add custom connector（新增自訂連接器）。名稱填 Parley，網址填 https://api.parley.tw/mcp，然後新增。
  3. 在 Parley 那一列點 Connect（連接）。Claude 會開一個 Parley 的頁面，接著進行下面的登入與授權。
  4. 回到對話，確認工具選單裡 Parley 是開啟的，就可以開始問了。

登入與授權畫面

1. 登入 Parley

如果這個瀏覽器還沒登入 Parley，會先看到 Parley 的登入頁。請用和 App 同一個帳號登入——用錯帳號，助理就會看到一個空的錄音庫。

2. 確認並允許

登入後會看到授權頁，標題寫著「「Claude」想讀取你的 Parley 錄音」，並列出它允許之後能做的事：

  • 列出你同步到雲端的錄音，以及你所屬組織分享的錄音
  • 讀取逐字稿與 Parley 的分析結果

頁面下方會寫出你目前的登入身分，以及授權完成後要回到的網址。確認名稱和網址都是你預期的助理，再按允許。按拒絕或關掉頁面，什麼都不會分享出去。

只在你自己發起連接時才按允許。如果你沒有在任何 AI 助理裡新增 Parley，卻看到這個授權頁，請按拒絕。

助理可以用的四個工具

工具做什麼什麼時候會用到
list_orgs列出你所屬的組織與角色。要讀團隊共享的錄音時，先拿到組織 ID。
list_recordings由新到舊列出錄音卡片：標題、日期、長度、講者數、重點與待辦數量、開頭片段、資料夾。可依標題篩選、限定資料夾或日期。「我上週錄了哪些會議？」
get_recording讀一份錄音的全部內容：標上講者與時間的完整逐字稿，加上 Parley 存下的摘要、重點與待辦。深入一場會議，或引用原話。
search_meetings跨錄音搜尋「說過的話」（每一行逐字稿，附 m:ss 時間）和「分析的結論」。一次掃最新的 60 份符合條件的錄音。「我們什麼時候談過價格？」「我答應過這個客戶什麼？」

可以這樣問

  • 「用 Parley 找出我這週所有會議裡答應別人的事，依對象分組，每一條附上會議名稱和時間點。」
  • 「搜尋我的 Parley 會議裡所有提到『報價』的地方，整理客戶的反應怎麼隨時間改變。」
  • 「讀最新一場和 ACME 的會議逐字稿，列出還沒回答的問題，幫我草擬一封後續追蹤信。」
  • 「列出我們組織共享資料夾裡上個月的錄音，挑出反覆出現的客戶疑慮。」

把範圍說清楚（哪個客戶、哪個資料夾、哪段時間）會比「幫我總結所有會議」準得多。重要的結論，請助理附上會議名稱與時間點，再回原逐字稿確認。

中斷連接

在 Claude 進入 Settings → Connectors，找到 Parley，選擇中斷連接（Disconnect）或移除。之後助理就無法再讀取你的錄音。

授權本身也會過期：存取權杖 1 小時、更新權杖 7 天；超過 7 天沒用，下次就要重新登入並允許。Parley 目前還沒有在 App 裡撤回授權的頁面，如果你想從 Parley 這端撤銷某個助理的存取，請來信 contact@pathors.com。資料怎麼處理，詳見隱私權政策。

Connect Parley to Claude or ChatGPT

Add one URL, sign in, click Allow, and your AI assistant can work through your meetings directly: who committed to what, which meetings covered a topic, where a customer got stuck last time. No exporting, and no pasting transcripts one at a time.

What it does

Parley Cloud runs a remote MCP server at https://api.parley.tw/mcp. Once you add it to an AI assistant, the assistant can list, search, and read the recordings you synced to Parley Cloud when you ask about them — including the full transcript labelled by speaker and time, and the analysis Parley saved (brief, findings, action items).

The connection is read-only: the assistant cannot change, delete, or add any recording. It reaches only your own recordings and the ones shared into organizations you belong to.

Not the same as the desktop app's local MCP. The desktop app has a built-in MCP server that runs only on your computer, for local tools such as Claude Code (see Meeting notes with MCP). The cloud connector needs no computer left running and works in Claude on the web and on your phone, but it sees only recordings that have synced to the cloud.

Before you start

  • A Parley Cloud account. The one you sign in to in the app, with email, Google, or Apple.
  • Recordings synced to the cloud. Recordings sync automatically once you are signed in to the app. A recording that exists only on a device, or has not finished uploading, is not visible to the assistant.
  • An AI assistant that supports custom connectors. Today that is Claude, on the web, desktop, or mobile. On Team or Enterprise plans an admin may need to add the connector first.

Add the connector in Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector. Enter Parley as the name and https://api.parley.tw/mcp as the URL, then add it.
  3. Click Connect next to Parley. Claude opens a Parley page for the sign-in and consent steps below.
  4. Back in a chat, check that Parley is turned on in the tools menu, and start asking.

The sign-in and consent screens

1. Sign in to Parley

If this browser is not signed in to Parley yet, you see Parley's sign-in page first. Sign in with the same account you use in the app — with a different account, the assistant sees an empty library.

2. Review and allow

Next comes the consent page. It names the assistant asking for access (for example, “Claude wants to read your Parley recordings”) and lists what it will be able to do:

  • List the recordings you synced to the cloud, and the ones shared in your organizations
  • Read transcripts and Parley's analysis

The bottom of the page shows who you are signed in as and the address you will return to afterwards. Check that both match the assistant you meant to connect, then click Allow. If you click Deny or close the page, nothing is shared. The page is currently shown in Traditional Chinese: Allow is 「允許」 and Deny is 「拒絕」.

Only click Allow for a connection you started. If you see this page without having added Parley to an AI assistant, click Deny.

The four tools the assistant can use

ToolWhat it doesWhen it comes up
list_orgsLists the organizations you belong to, with your role.Getting an organization ID before reading team-shared recordings.
list_recordingsLists recording cards, newest first: title, date, length, speaker count, finding and action-item counts, opening snippet, folder. Can filter by title, folder, or date.“Which meetings did I record last week?”
get_recordingReads one recording in full: the complete transcript labelled by speaker and time, plus the brief, findings, and action items Parley saved.Going deep on one meeting, or quoting it.
search_meetingsSearches what was said (every transcript line, with an m:ss timestamp) and what the analysis concluded, across recordings. Each search reads the 60 newest matching recordings.“When did we discuss pricing?” “What did I promise this customer?”

Things to ask

  • “Use Parley to find everything I committed to in this week's meetings, grouped by person, each with the meeting name and timestamp.”
  • “Search my Parley meetings for every mention of the quote, and show how the customer's reaction changed over time.”
  • “Read the transcript of my latest meeting with ACME, list the questions we left unanswered, and draft a follow-up email.”
  • “List last month's recordings in our organization's shared folders and pick out the customer concerns that keep coming back.”

A clear scope (a customer, a folder, a time range) works far better than “summarize all my meetings”. For conclusions that matter, ask the assistant to cite the meeting and timestamp, and check them against the transcript.

Disconnect

In Claude, open Settings → Connectors, find Parley, and choose Disconnect or remove it. The assistant can no longer read your recordings after that.

The authorization also expires on its own: access tokens last 1 hour and refresh tokens 7 days, so after 7 days without use you have to sign in and allow it again. Parley does not have an in-app page for revoking access yet; to revoke an assistant's access from Parley's side, email contact@pathors.com. The privacy policy covers how the data is handled.

What about ChatGPT?

ChatGPT support is coming. When it is available, this guide will add the steps for adding Parley in ChatGPT; it uses the same URL and the same sign-in and consent flow.
